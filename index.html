The next generation of engineers starts with one kit.

Future Spark is a student-run nonprofit that designs, assembles, and delivers free hands-on STEM kits to under-resourced elementary schools, so kids get to try engineering before deciding it isn't for them.

Our story

It started as a class project. It turned into 300 kits.

We're high school students from Columbus, Ohio. Future Spark began as a service project for an English class, where we were asked to bridge gaps for marginalized students in our community.

We ask kids what they want to be when they grow up, but a student can decide they're not "an engineering person" without ever building a circuit, designing a bridge, or knowing what an engineer actually does.

I didn't get interested in engineering because someone explained the definition to me. I liked making things, messing something up, figuring out what went wrong, and trying again.The Future Spark team

We don't expect one STEM kit to convince every student to become an engineer. But at least they got to try it first.
